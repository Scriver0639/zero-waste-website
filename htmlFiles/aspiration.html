My Zero Waste Journey

Goals, challenges, and plans to overcome challenges in order to achieve zero waste living

My Zero Waste Goals

  • Replace single-use plastic water bottles with a reusable water bottle
  • Using reusable bags instead of plastic bags
  • Reducing food wastes by only preparing the amount of food I can finish
  • Using reusable containers instead of plastic containers

Challenges I've Faced

  • It's hard to remember to use reusable bags as it has already become a habit to use plastic bags
  • It is hard to find reusable containers that are affordable and sometimes I am lazy to wash it
  • It's hard to be consistent in practicing the ways daily

Plans to Overcome Challenges

  • Put a reusable bag in my backpack at all times so I won't forget to use it
  • Make a list of practices and paste it on my wall. This is to remind me to practise zero waste living everyday
  • Only take/cook what I can finish so I do not waste food
