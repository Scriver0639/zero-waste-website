About Me

The person behind the zero waste journey

Who I am

Hello! I'm Nicholas, an environmental enthusiast and I'm on a mission to live a zero waste lifestyle. I am very passionate about reducing waste and promoting sustainable living practices. Therefore, I have created this website to share my zero waste journey and share some tips and tricks that I have tried.


My Passion for Eco-Friendly Living

My passion for eco-friendly living stems from my desire to make a positive impact on the environment. I am a firm believer that small changes in our daily lives can lead to significant improvements in the health of our planet. Through this website, I aim to inspire others to embrace sustainable practices and reduce their ecological footprint through a zero waste lifestyle.


My Experience with Waste Reduction

Over the past few years, I have been actively working towards reducing my waste and living a more sustainable lifestyle. From using reusable bags and containers to repairing items myself, I have learned a lot about the importance of waste reduction. Besides that, I am also actively trying my best to recycle whenever possible to minimise the amount of waste I produce.


My Favourite "Green Hacks" I've Tried

I'm always on the lookout for new "green hacks" to incorporate into my daily routine. Some of my favourites include:

  • Bringing my own water bottle everywhere
  • Reuse plastic containers that I have gotten from takeaway meals
  • Using lemons, vinegar, baking soda, etc, to make cleaning products
